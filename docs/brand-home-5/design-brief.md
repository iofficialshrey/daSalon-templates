# Oru Spa: Brand Home 05

## Identity

- Brand: Oru Spa (fictional), an urban spa in Singapore
- Concept: The art of exhale
- Emotional register: human, quietly artistic, restorative and precise
- Revamped November 2026 to the standard of templates 1 to 4

## Visual system

- Porcelain `#f3f0ed`, chalk `#fffdf8`, lavender mist `#ddd6e6`
- Aubergine `#2d2030` for ink and the night bands
- Eucalyptus `#7f9b90` (status), vermilion `#eb654f` (the one warm accent; `#c2452f` on light)
- Display type: Bodoni Moda (next/font, optical sizes), with its italic as the second voice. Manrope for everything else.
- Shapes: pills, arches (after the niches in the photographs) and generous radii. No small capital labels.

## Structure

1. Hero: the photograph, the line and "How long do you have?" (30, 60, 90 minutes). Sticky: the page rises over it.
2. Treatment finder: choose a room and a window of time; the room's live menu narrows to fit. Every row books.
3. The house: the point of view, with the wide photograph walking from pool to daybeds on scroll.
4. Four rooms: each a live venue with status, a one-line menu summary, Book and Treatments.
5. Membership, journeys and gift cards: three tabs, three cards each, with a demonstration checkout.
6. Closing invitation and footer.

## Motion

Lenis smooths mouse and trackpad scrolling. Reveals, the hero's dusk and the house photograph's walk are CSS scroll-driven animations; where unsupported, or with reduced motion, everything is simply in place.
