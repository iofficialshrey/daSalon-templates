# Néroli House: Brand Home 06

## Identity

- Brand: Néroli House (fictional), a mineral-water spa in Singapore
- Line: Come back to your senses.
- Revamped November 2026. Kept: the name, the water-and-glass idea, the palette, the three photographs and the travelling glass drop.

## The drop

One glass drop travels down the page and rests in places the layout keeps empty for it, so it never covers text. It moves on a spring: it lags a little, stretches into a teardrop in the direction it travels, wobbles as it settles, and casts a soft shadow only when resting. It hides while a booking or checkout window is open.

Resting places, in order:

1. Hero: the full stop of "Come back to your senses."
2. Treatments: inside the chosen category bubble (it follows your choice).
3. A day at Néroli: on the waterline under the three panels, rolling from Settle to Return as they slide past.
4. Houses: beside the house you are looking at (it follows hover and focus).
5. Membership: under the chosen tab.
6. Closing: it falls into the deep water and the ripples open around Book.

Reduced motion: no travelling drop; each resting place shows a still drop.

## Visual system

- Mineral `#eef3f0`, mist `#dce8e5`, celadon `#b9d2ca`, pool `#86b9b5`, deep `#18302e`, abyss `#0f2321`, coral `#e56f61` (`#b94a3c` for text on light).
- The page descends: light at the surface, celadon in the middle, deep green-black at the end.
- Display type: Fraunces with its soft axis turned up (round, liquid). Manrope for everything else. No italic second voice, no small labels.
- Shapes: circles and soft rectangles (14px buttons, 24px panels), glass surfaces.

## Sections

1. Hero: copy left; a round lens of the arrival photograph right.
2. Treatments: category bubbles sized by how many treatments each has; a house switch; tiles that book.
3. A day at Néroli: Settle, Restore, Return as three panels that slide sideways while the page scrolls (stacked on phones).
4. Houses: four rows; the hovered house shows its photograph in a round lens.
5. Membership, rituals, gifts: glass membership cards, ritual cards drawn as layers of water, gift cards as postcards; demonstration checkout.
6. Closing and footer: deep water, ripples, Book.
