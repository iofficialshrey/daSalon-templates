# Brand Home 4 design brief

## Identity

- Brand: Paloma
- Position: expressive hair, form and colour studio
- Point of view: a salon website composed like a living fashion issue
- Palette: porcelain, ink, cobalt and pale blue only
- Typography: bold modern grotesk, tightly tracked display type, compact editorial labels

## Animation mode

`animated-website`

The hero behaves as one continuous editorial shot. Scroll shifts the image crop, headline and issue marker at different speeds. Subsequent motion is typographic and graphic rather than cinematic, preserving a fast and confident experience.

## Sections

1. Cover story hero
2. Moving manifesto
3. Service editions
4. Seasonal package grid
5. Studio method and object study
6. Membership and loyalty ledger
7. Gift card configurator
8. Location directory
9. Studio note
10. Booking flow
