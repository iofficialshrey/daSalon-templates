# Brand Home 4 design brief

## Identity

- Brand: Paloma
- Position: expressive hair, form and colour studio
- Point of view: a salon website composed like a living fashion issue
- Palette: porcelain, ink, cobalt and pale blue only
- Typography: bold modern grotesk, tightly tracked display type, compact editorial labels

## Animation mode

`animated-website`

The hero behaves as one continuous editorial shot. Scroll shifts the image crop, headline and issue marker at different speeds. A live issue index tracks the reader through the page, while oversized chapter numerals, staggered rows and a kinetic type interlude keep subsequent motion typographic and graphic rather than cinematic.
