# Brand Home 6 design brief

## Identity

- Brand: Néroli House
- Position: a contemporary mineral-water spa for unhurried restoration
- Design read: luminous, intelligent calm for city guests who value ritual without wellness clichés
- Concept spine: one water pearl travels through the house, becoming a droplet, marker, seal, and open lotus
- Delivery tier: cinema, expressed through one shared object rather than a generated film
- Status: approved final template, included in the Brand Home collection

## Locked palette

- Mineral: `#eef3f0`
- Celadon: `#b9d2ca`
- Pool: `#86b9b5`
- Ink: `#18302e`
- Mist: `#dce8e5`
- Coral: `#e56f61`

The palette takes its character from water, glass, and neroli blossom. Coral is used only as a directional accent.

## Locked typography

- Display: Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Baskerville, Georgia
- Utility and body: Manrope, Avenir Next, Arial

The serif introduces quiet editorial elegance while the restrained grotesk keeps treatment information practical.

## Animation mode

`animated-website`

The page uses one persistent glass pearl whose position, scale, droplet shape, rotation, and lotus bloom are scrubbed between measured section anchors. Supporting images, divider lines, and type move at a gentler rate. Mobile receives local static pearl markers and reduced motion receives a composed still experience.

## Section plan

1. Arrival image with editorial hero
2. Treatment index with image counterpoint
3. Three-part day ritual
4. House membership and recovery lounge
5. Gift ritual configurator
6. Guest note and locations
7. Final booking invitation

## CTA inventory

- Begin your visit: outlined circular-arrow action
- Explore treatments: text and growing rule action
- Reserve a ritual: filled coral capsule
- Enter the House: framed celadon invitation
- Send a ritual: folding gift-card action
- Book Néroli: oversized final pearl action
