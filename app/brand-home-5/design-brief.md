# Oru Spa: Brand Home 05

Animation mode: animated-website

## Concept

Oru is a contemporary urban spa built around the transition from sensory noise to quiet attention. The page behaves as one uninterrupted environment. A chalky celadon ground persists from navigation through booking, while film, typography, and thin botanical contours move across it in a single calm current.

The Get Heaven reference contributes only high-level principles: one continuous background, generous spacing, restrained interface chrome, and interaction embedded into the page flow. No branding, copy, layouts, or product motifs are copied.

## Combinatorial direction

- Theme paradigm: Bold Studio Solid, softened into mineral celadon
- Background character: solid with soft ambient depth
- Typography character: refined grotesk with a narrow mono-style utility voice
- Hero architecture: massive image-first composition with restrained offset type
- Section system: asymmetric premium flow
- Signature components: vertical rhythm lines, off-grid editorial copy, layered image crop frames, hover accordion slices
- Narrative spine: living system and garden
- Second-read moment: one oversized circular punctuation form used as a breathing aperture

## Palette

- Ground: `#dbe6dc`, a cool celadon mineral wash
- Deep ink: `#17332b`, a forest charcoal
- Muted ink: `#52665f`
- Light veil: `#eef3ee`
- Single accent: `#e55e6c`, a warm camellia pink used only for active states and focal details

This palette comes from steamed eucalyptus, mineral clay, and one fresh camellia petal. It intentionally avoids the cream, brass, espresso luxury default and differs from Brand Home 04's black, porcelain, cobalt system.

## Type

- Display and body: Manrope variable, with low contrast weights and compressed tracking for an architectural grotesk feel
- Utility labels: the same family in uppercase with increased tracking, used sparingly
- No serif. The identity should feel contemporary, sensory, and precise rather than heritage-coded.

## Journey shape

- Shape: `single-shot`
- Film: one existing project-owned spa entrance film, scrubbed directly by page scroll with a celadon grade and animated aperture treatment
- Camera: a slow forward architectural arrival that becomes an abstract threshold inside the Oru visual system
- Start: closed mineral aperture around the spa threshold
- Middle: aperture opens and reveals more architectural depth
- End: the film resolves into a wide quiet threshold before the page returns to the continuous celadon canvas
- Motion contract: scroll controls film time, aperture scale, and light movement with damped requestAnimationFrame updates
- Mobile: center-safe crop using the same local film, no separate download or paid generation
- Delivery budget: reuse the existing local film with no additional transfer asset

## Section plan

1. Sticky film journey with four semantic chapters and route navigation
2. Treatments as a full-width hover accordion, no card grid
3. House method as an oversized circular breathing aperture with an offset text ledger
4. Membership as a layered editorial crop frame with a simple comparison table
5. Locations as two generous divided rows
6. Booking finale on the same celadon ground with a distinct camellia CTA garment

## CTA inventory

- Book now: nav label that parts to reveal a moving camellia stroke
- View treatments: first-chapter text band whose whole baseline shifts on hover
- Choose a ritual: accordion rows expand and disclose duration and price
- Join Oru House: circular aperture hit area that gently tightens on press
- Book now finale: large offset camellia block that slides its label rather than flood-filling

## Asset plan and credit decision

- The user declined further paid generation after selecting the animated direction.
- Reuse the project-owned `/assets/spa-entrance.mp4` as the single scrub film.
- Reuse its local threshold still as the film poster and gallery cover.
- Build the monogram, treatment symbols, background atmosphere, and page chrome with HTML and CSS.
- No additional Higgsfield jobs or credits are authorized.
